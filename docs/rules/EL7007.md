# EL7007 — edifact.service-string

| Class | Severity | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| edifact | error | `edifact` |

UNA is not the fixed nine characters, its service characters collide or are alphanumeric, or its decimal mark is not a period or a comma.

## What it catches

UNA is not the fixed nine characters, its service characters collide or are alphanumeric, or its decimal mark is not a period or a comma.

## Example

This input is a minimal illustration of the defect:

```text
UNB+...'
# edifact.service-string
```

The CLI reports:

```text
example.edi: error: [EL7007 edifact.service-string] UNA is not the fixed nine characters, its service characters collide or are alphanumeric, or its decimal mark is not a period or a comma.
```

## How to fix

Regenerate the envelope from the payload, preserving its nesting and recalculating trailer values and control references.

## When to suppress

Suppress this rule only when the receiving system explicitly accepts the condition and changing the source data is not possible. Prefer a narrow rule suppression for the affected file; record the receiver requirement alongside it.
